# Client Outreach Prompt — YouTube Channel Management Niche

## Target Client Profile
Busy professionals, entrepreneurs, coaches, consultants, or business owners who:
- Have a YouTube channel but post inconsistently or have gone quiet
- Have an existing audience or income stream (not starting from zero)
- Want to grow on YouTube, Instagram Reels, or TikTok
- Don't have time to edit, upload, or manage content themselves

---

## AI Prompt — Finding & Messaging Target Clients

Use this prompt in ChatGPT, Claude, or any AI outreach tool:

---

> You are an expert outreach strategist for a premium freelance video editor and channel manager. Your task is to help find and write personalized cold outreach messages targeting a very specific client type.
>
> **Target client profile:**
> A YouTube creator (or aspiring creator) who has a primary income source outside of content — such as a business owner, coach, consultant, real estate investor, fitness trainer, lawyer, doctor, or entrepreneur — who started or wants to grow a YouTube/social media presence but struggles to stay consistent due to their busy schedule.
>
> They typically show one or more of these signals:
> - YouTube channel with fewer than 50K subscribers but good content quality
> - Posting gaps of 2–6+ weeks between videos
> - Says things like "I need to be more consistent" or "I know I should post more"
> - Has an audience on another platform (Instagram, podcast, LinkedIn) but underdeveloped YouTube
> - Sells a course, coaching program, product, or service — YouTube is their growth lever
>
> **My service:**
> I am Biniyam, a professional video editor and social media content manager based in Addis Ababa, Ethiopia. I have 4+ years of experience, 200+ projects completed, 50M+ views generated, and a 98% client satisfaction rate. I specialize in:
> - Turning raw footage or recorded calls into cinematic, high-retention YouTube videos
> - Creating short-form clips (Reels, Shorts, TikToks) from long-form content
> - Managing the full post-production pipeline so the client only needs to film
> - Pricing from $400/month on retainer
>
> **Task 1 — Where to find them:**
> List 10 specific places online where this type of client hangs out, posts, or can be found (e.g. specific subreddits, Facebook groups, LinkedIn hashtags, Twitter/X communities, YouTube comment sections, podcast communities, etc.).
>
> **Task 2 — Write 3 personalized cold DM/email templates:**
> Each message should:
> - Open with a specific observation about THEIR channel or content (leave a placeholder like [OBSERVATION])
> - Identify the exact pain point without being pushy
> - Position my service as the solution naturally
> - Be short — under 120 words
> - End with a low-pressure call to action (not "book a call", something softer)
> - Sound like a real human, not a pitch bot
>
> Write one version for: (a) Instagram DM, (b) YouTube comment → DM follow-up, (c) cold email.
>
> **Task 3 — Write a follow-up message:**
> A short, friendly 1-paragraph follow-up to send 4–5 days after no reply. Not desperate. Just a gentle nudge that adds value (share a quick tip, result, or relevant insight).
>
> **Task 4 — Write my outreach bio/pitch line:**
> A single sentence I can put in my DM profile or outreach opener that instantly communicates my value to this niche. Something like: "I help [target] do [outcome] without [pain point]."

## Quick One-Liner Pitch (use in bios, DMs, intro lines)

> "I help busy entrepreneurs and coaches grow their YouTube channel and social media — without them spending a single hour on editing or uploads."

---

## Platforms to Target First

| Platform | What to Search / Where to Look |
|----------|-------------------------------|
| YouTube | Channels 1K–50K subs, niche: business/finance/fitness/coaching, last post 3+ weeks ago |
| Instagram | Coaches/consultants with 10K+ followers but weak YouTube presence |
| LinkedIn | Entrepreneurs posting video content manually, looking for scale |
| Twitter/X | Creators tweeting about "consistency" or "need to post more" |
| Reddit | r/EntrepreneurRideAlong, r/youtubers, r/SmallYTChannel |
| Facebook Groups | "YouTube Growth", "Online Coaches & Consultants", niche business groups |
| Podcasts | Podcast hosts who haven't cross-posted to YouTube |
| Skool / Circle | Course creators and community owners |

---

## Red Flags to Avoid
- Channels with zero content (no budget mindset yet)
- Creators who edit themselves and take pride in it (wrong fit)
- Anyone asking for free trials upfront
- Channels in saturated niches with no monetization path
