# CV Design Prompt

## For use with: Canva AI / Adobe Firefly / Figma AI / ChatGPT (image) / Midjourney / v0.dev / design AI tools

---

Design a **premium, cinematic single-page CV** for a high-end freelance video editor and motion designer. The design should feel like it belongs in a creative agency portfolio, not a generic job board.

### Visual Identity
- **Color palette:** Deep black (#0a0a0a) background with electric cyan (#00f5ff) or neon amber (#ffb703) as the accent. White (#ffffff) for primary text, mid-gray (#888888) for secondary text.
- **Typography:** Use a bold condensed sans-serif for the name/headers (e.g. Bebas Neue, Monument Extended, or Neue Haas Grotesk). Clean light sans-serif for body text (e.g. Inter, DM Sans).
- **Aesthetic:** Dark mode, cinematic, editorial — think film credits meets tech agency. Subtle grain texture or noise overlay on the background. Optional: thin horizontal rules between sections.
- **Mood:** Confident, premium, creative — not flashy, not corporate.

### Layout Structure (single page, A4 or US Letter)
1. **Header (top)** — Full name in large bold type: BINIYAM GETACHEW TEKA. Below it: subtitle line "Video Editor · Motion Designer · Post-Production Specialist". Far right or below: location (Addis Ababa, Ethiopia), availability badge ("Open to Remote Work"), and a clean accent-colored "Available for Work" pill/tag.

2. **Stats bar** — A horizontal band with 4 key numbers in large type: `50M+ Views` · `200+ Projects` · `4.9★ Rating` · `98% Satisfaction`. Use accent color for the numbers, white for the labels.

3. **Two-column body:**
   - **Left column (narrower, ~35%):**
     - Skills section with horizontal bar charts or percentage arcs for: Premiere Pro 95%, After Effects 90%, DaVinci Resolve 88%, Audition 85%, Photoshop 82%, Cinema 4D 75%
     - Software logos/icons row
     - Core services list (Retention Engineering, Long-Form Narrative, Motion Graphics, Color & Sound)
     - Contact/links section at the bottom

   - **Right column (wider, ~65%):**
     - Professional Summary (2–3 lines, elegant)
     - Experience block: "Biniyam Edits — Freelance Video Editor & Motion Designer, 2020–Present" with 4–5 bullet points of key achievements
     - Career milestones as a vertical timeline: 2020 → 2021 → 2022 → 2023 → 2024, each with a short label
     - One testimonial quote styled as a pull quote with accent color quotation marks

4. **Footer** — Thin accent-colored line, then: portfolio URL · LinkedIn · Telegram, centered or left-aligned.

### Design Details
- Use generous whitespace — never cramped
- Section labels in small caps or uppercase tracking, accent color
- Skill bars should be sleek and minimal (not cartoonish)
- Subtle gradient or glow behind the name in the header
- Optional: a very faint film strip or waveform graphic element as a background watermark
- No profile photo needed — the design itself should be the identity statement

### Output
Deliver a clean, print-ready A4 layout. Export-ready at 300dpi if for print, 1x/2x PNG if digital. Must look equally sharp on screen and printed in black & white.
